# MILES.md — the producer's notes

Miles reads this at the start of every job (like CLAUDE.md for Claude Code). Taste, rules, defaults: edit freely.

## Taste (learned from the "good beats" playlist, 2026-10-15)

- When a job names no style, lean this way: warm, dark, melodic; R&B / neo-soul / piano and guitar ballads (Frank Ocean, Daniel Caesar, SZA, Joji, d4vd, Mac Miller), lofi / jazz (Nujabes), city pop; some modern R&B-trap (Brent Faiyaz, 4batz, Travis / Carti).
- Tempo: the felt pulse is slow — median 76 BPM, middle half 65-94 (38 % of the set is written at 120+ for a half-time feel). Ballads 60-75.
- Sounds: Rhodes, piano, acoustic / electric guitar, sax; a deep bass or 808 under almost everything (bass in 89 % of the set). Vinyl crackle is fine; heavy crushing / cassette wobble isn't the taste.
- Mix: drums forward but not loud, the melody well under the drums, a deep low end. Clean, polished, not muddy: one tone move per beat unless asked.
- 18 % of the set has no drums and 20 % soft drums: a ballad can skip the kit or keep it very sparse; swing is nearly straight.
